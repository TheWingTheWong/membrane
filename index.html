IDiscretise

Your outline becomes a 64×64 grid. Each cell inside the drum is an unknown displacement, and the rim is clamped to zero. The wave equation’s spatial part, −∇²u = λu, turns into a sparse matrix with about 3,000 rows.

IISolve

A banded Cholesky factorisation and shift-invert block Krylov iteration find the 24 lowest eigenpairs in a fraction of a second, all in a Web Worker. Each eigenvector is a mode shape and each √λ is a frequency.

IIIListen

A strike excites every mode in proportion to how much it moves at that spot. Strike a nodal line and that mode stays silent. The modes ring and decay, the highest ones first, and their sum is the sound you hear.
